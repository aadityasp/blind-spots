# Privacy policy

Blind spots is a Claude Code mod that runs entirely inside your Claude Code session. It has no server, collects no analytics and keeps no data of its own outside your session.

## What it reads

After each of Claude's replies in your main conversation, the mod forks that conversation once through Claude Code and asks Claude for two short lists. The fork goes to the same Claude model your session already uses, under the same terms as the rest of your conversation. The mod sends nothing to any other service.

## What it keeps

The two lists and the items you've pressed are kept in the mod's state inside your Claude Code session. The mod doesn't write them anywhere else or send them anywhere.

## What it sends

The mod submits a prompt only when you press an item in the pane. The text it submits is the one-sentence message shown for that item, and it goes into your conversation as your next message. The mod never submits anything on its own.

## Personal data and children

The mod doesn't ask for, collect or store personal data such as names, emails or addresses. It isn't intended for people under 18.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the issue tracker at https://github.com/aadityasp/blind-spots/issues.
